<h1>💓 Heartbeat Portal</h1>
<blockquote>
<p>Monitor all your ECS clusters and services—<strong>across environments, at a glance</strong>.</p>
</blockquote>
<p><a href="https://github.com/yogeshp-code/HeartBeat">Source Code</a></p>
<h2>🧠 Why Heartbeat?</h2>
<p>As a DevOps engineer, I often found myself facing these recurring problems:</p>
<ul>
<li>💤 <strong>Laziness &amp; inefficiency</strong>: Opening each ECS cluster individually on the AWS Console just to check running tasks.</li>
<li>🔁 <strong>Misconfigurations</strong>: Sometimes due to incorrect deployment or migration, services ran more tasks than required, costing more and adding chaos.</li>
<li>📉 <strong>Poor observability</strong>: Even with tools like Grafana or SigNoz, I needed to manually select the cluster and service from dropdowns—<strong>again and again</strong>—just to get basic information.</li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p>In short: <strong>Too many clicks, not enough visibility.</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<h2>🚀 The Solution: Heartbeat Portal</h2>
<p>I envisioned a unified view—where I could:</p>
<p>✅ See the real-time running task count for <strong>all services in all ECS clusters</strong><br>✅ Monitor configuration drift or unexpected behavior (like over-provisioning)<br>✅ Avoid jumping between AWS Console, Grafana, or dashboards  </p>
<p>And so, <strong>Heartbeat Portal</strong> was born.</p>
<hr>
<h3>⚡ Plug &amp; Play, Minimal Cost Monitoring</h3>
<p>All of this at <strong>unnoticeable AWS cost</strong>.<br>Just plug and play — configure your AWS profiles and <strong>see the magic</strong> ✨</p>
<hr>
<h2>🛠️ Tech Stack</h2>
<p>I learned backend, frontend, and microservices in college, so I leveraged that knowledge and curiosity to build this portal.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Frontend</strong>: <a href="https://nextjs.org/">Next.js</a> + React</li>
<li><strong>Backend</strong>: <a href="https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/">FastAPI</a></li>
<li><strong>Deployment</strong>: Containerized for portability</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2>✨ Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>🔍 View <strong>running ECS tasks</strong> across all clusters &amp; services</li>
<li>🌍 Supports <strong>multi-environment visibility</strong></li>
<li>💡 Detect <strong>misconfigurations</strong> or <strong>auto-scaling drifts</strong></li>
<li>🧩 Plug &amp; play architecture (can be extended to Lambda or other services in future)</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2>🔮 Future Possibilities</h2>
<p>Heartbeat is designed to be extensible. In future iterations, we plan to extend support beyond ECS to other AWS services like Lambda, SQS, RDS etc.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>The vision is to evolve Heartbeat into a unified, low-cost monitoring dashboard for all critical AWS workloads.</p>
</blockquote>
<hr>

<h2>💭 Inspiration</h2>
<p>The idea was simple: <strong>make ECS monitoring simple enough to fit in one screen.</strong><br>Driven by laziness (yes, really), curiosity, and the day-to-day pain of scattered observability—Heartbeat is my answer to centralized, practical, and usable ECS insight.</p>
